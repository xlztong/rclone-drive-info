netcup-rclone

A personal Google Drive utility operated by xlztong, using the open-source rclone command-line application on a privately managed server.

Purpose

The utility lets its owner copy accessible Google Drive files into their own Drive, upload selected files, and download selected files or folders to their server. It is for personal use and does not offer a public file-transfer service.

Google Drive access

Google OAuth authorizes the utility to access the owner's Drive. Read and write permissions support listing, copying, uploading, downloading, renaming, and deleting files when the owner requests those operations. The owner can revoke access through their Google Account at any time.
